# Use of the name "Perdrix"

*[Version française](NAME-POLICY.fr.md)*

The norm is free to use, translate and adapt (CC BY 4.0), but the licence **does not grant the name**. This page says when the name "Perdrix" may be used. It is the author's statement of terms, not legal advice; the name is not, at the time of writing, a registered trademark.

## What the name designates

- **Perdrix** is the open norm published in this repository, in the versions published by its author (Hadrien Diakonoff, <https://perdrix-dvi.org/>).
- **Perdrix-DVI** is the application of the same author that applies it.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that a record **conforms to Perdrix x.y** when it follows the format, the vocabularies and the rules of the **unmodified** norm of version x.y as published here.
- Saying that an application is **compatible with Perdrix x.y** when it reads and writes such records and applies the **unmodified** norm of version x.y.
- Citing, quoting and linking the norm, with the credit given in the README.
- Publishing a **faithful translation**, marked as an unofficial translation, with the credit.
- Using the norm's identifiers, values and rules in an application or a document, with the credit.

Conformity is **self-declared**: there is no certification and no public validator, and the author does not vouch for it.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Publishing a **modified version** of the norm (values, rules, structure added, removed or changed) **under the name Perdrix**, or under a name that can be mistaken for it ("Perdrix 2", "Perdrix+", "New Perdrix"…).
- Saying or letting it be understood that a modified version, an application or a service is **the author's, approved or endorsed by the author**, or by Interpol.
- Presenting oneself as the **author or the origin** of the norm.

## If you modify the norm

You may, under CC BY 4.0: publish your version **under another name**, say that it is **based on Perdrix by Hadrien Diakonoff** with a link, **indicate your changes**, and keep the licence notice. A national or institutional **extension** that adds its own values without changing those of the norm is welcome: give it its own name and its own identifiers, and say which version of Perdrix it extends.
